What is CashCat Swap?

CashCat Swap means exchanging CASHCAT — a community memecoin — against ETH through a decentralized exchange rather than an order book. CASHCAT is an ERC-20 token with a fixed supply of 1 billion and no mint function, deployed on Robinhood Chain shortly after that chain's 1 July 2026 mainnet launch.

The largest onchain venue is the Uniswap V3 CASHCAT/WETH pool, where a pool contract quotes output from its reserves and your trade size. Your wallet authorizes the public smart-contract transaction, so no operator holds your account or keys. This is an independent dashboard, and CASHCAT is not affiliated with or endorsed by Robinhood — the project describes itself as "fan fiction with a ticker."

How CashCat Swap works

To swap, connect an EVM wallet set to Robinhood Chain, choose the input token (ETH) and output token (CASHCAT) — or the reverse — and enter an amount. The live app displays the Uniswap V3 route, expected output, price impact, slippage tolerance, the pool fee, and estimated gas before you sign.

Buying CASHCAT with the chain's native ETH needs only gas. Selling CASHCAT first requires a one-time token approval for the router, which is a separate transaction. The swap then enforces a minimum received: price impact describes the quoted execution, while slippage tolerance controls how far execution may move before the transaction reverts.

Robinhood Chain & where it runs

CASHCAT lives on Robinhood Chain, an EVM-compatible Ethereum layer 2 built with Arbitrum technology and launched on 1 July 2026. Add the network to your wallet and hold ETH on Robinhood Chain to pay gas; onchain swaps execute against the Uniswap V3 CASHCAT/WETH pool.

The CASHCAT token contract is 0x020bfC650A365f8BB26819deAAbF3E21291018b4. A centralized alternative is MEXC's CASHCAT/USDT market, which skips onchain gas but is custodial. A swap stays on Robinhood Chain; moving CASHCAT to another network requires a separate bridge transaction.

The CASHCAT/WETH pool

The primary onchain liquidity for CASHCAT is the CASHCAT/WETH pool on Uniswap V3, using the 1% fee tier. Because it pairs against wrapped ETH, buying CASHCAT with native ETH routes through WETH automatically inside the swap.

Memecoin liquidity is thin relative to major tokens, so a large order can move the pool price and produce high price impact. Select from the actual token contract rather than the ticker alone, and check the pool depth in the live app before sizing a trade — a route existing does not mean the fill will be efficient.

Fees, gas & price impact

A CASHCAT swap's cost is the Uniswap V3 pool fee (the CASHCAT/WETH pool uses the 1% tier), network gas paid in ETH on Robinhood Chain, and the economic loss represented by price impact. Price impact is an execution effect from available liquidity, not a protocol fee.

Selling CASHCAT can add a one-time ERC-20 approval transaction that consumes gas separately. Compare the expected output and the minimum received against the fee display, and keep enough ETH on Robinhood Chain to cover both the approval and the swap.

Is CASHCAT safe?

CASHCAT is a high-risk, volatile memecoin created by an anonymous developer with no Robinhood affiliation — treat any purchase as speculative and not investment advice. The contract has a fixed 1 billion supply with no mint function and is verified on public explorers, but that does not make a trade risk-free.

Smart-contract bugs, broad token approvals, thin-liquidity price swings, compromised front ends, and phishing all remain real risks. Before signing, verify the network, the token contract 0x020bfC…018b4, the pool, the spender, the allowance amount, the expected output, and the minimum received.

Swap problems & fixes

CASHCAT swap failures and poor fills usually trace to a network mismatch, missing gas or approval, thin liquidity, or a quote that moved before confirmation.

  • Wrong network: set both the wallet and app to Robinhood Chain, then rebuild the quote.
  • No gas: keep enough ETH on Robinhood Chain for the approval and swap.
  • Missing approval: when selling CASHCAT, wait for the approval to confirm and check that its spender matches the quote.
  • High price impact: CASHCAT liquidity is thin — reduce or split the order rather than widening slippage.
  • Reverted transaction: refresh the route, since the minimum received, deadline, or pool state may have changed. A reverted transaction can still consume gas.
